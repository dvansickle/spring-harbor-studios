Paredo for QuickBooks® Online

A calmer QuickBooks workspace.

Bookkeeping deserves your attention. The clutter around it doesn’t.

Paredo gives selected QuickBooks Online screens a cleaner layout, clearer tables, and controls you can reveal when you need them. Keep working in the QuickBooks you know, with less competing for your attention.

Less in the way

Hide supported promotions, suggestions, and secondary tools so your day-to-day bookkeeping has more room.

A clearer view of your books

Reduce space taken by headers and account cards, and make supported tables easier to read. Use Paredo across Home, Bank Transactions, Invoices, selected Reports, and other supported screens.

Your workspace, your choice

Start with Paredo’s defaults, then choose what to show on each supported screen. Display preferences stay in your Chrome profile across companies. Switch Paredo off for a screen whenever you want the native layout.

Your financial data stays with QuickBooks

Paredo processes supported pages locally to adjust their display. It does not send QuickBooks page content, transaction details, balances, or customer records to Spring Harbor Studios or our billing providers. The extension has no analytics or advertising trackers and does not request QuickBooks API access.
