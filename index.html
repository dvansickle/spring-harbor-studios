Small, useful web tools for specific interests

Spring Harbor Studios makes focused tools that solve a clear problem without adding unnecessary complexity. Our current projects help people protect time, plan visually, and keep watch over places they care about.

About

Based in Madison, Wisconsin, Spring Harbor Studios is an independent developer creating straightforward web utilities. Our projects are intentionally compact, easy to use, and clear about how they handle your data. For questions or support, email support@springharborstudios.com.

Our Tools

Google Calendar™ add-on

Windblock

Watches the wind forecast and automatically protects matching hours in a dedicated Google Calendar™. Choose the location, wind threshold, active hours, and forecast horizon.

  • Uses hourly Visual Crossing forecasts
  • Refreshes every four hours
  • Free, with no separate account

Chrome™ extension

YearMap

A year-at-a-glance planner for Chrome™ with range selection, category lanes, notes, day totals, and local ICS export.

  • Runs in your new tab or a regular tab
  • Chrome storage with optional device sync
  • No account or extension tracking
Add to Chrome Learn more
Weather monitoring

Yesterday's Weather

Monitors cabins, second homes, rentals, and other places from afar using observed weather and alerts rather than forecasts.

  • NOAA observations and NWS alerts
  • Daily or notable-events-only email
  • Up to five saved US locations
