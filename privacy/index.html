Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 11, 2026

Spring Harbor Studios builds focused web utilities and aims to collect only the information each product needs. We do not sell personal data. Questions can be sent to support@springharborstudios.com.

Who we are

Spring Harbor Studios is an independent developer based in Madison, Wisconsin, USA.

Products covered

  • Windblock — a Google Calendar™ add-on.
  • YearMap — a Chrome™ extension.
  • Yesterday's Weather — a web application and optional weather email service.

Windblock

Windblock uses permissions you approve to create and manage a dedicated Windblock calendar and matching events in your Google Account™. Windblock's weather forecasts are provided by Visual Crossing using your saved location and rules. Windblock does not read the contents of your personal calendar events, sell personal data, or maintain a separate customer account. Its settings and calendar data remain within Google services.

YearMap

YearMap runs within your Chrome profile. Calendar ranges, categories, notes, and settings are stored using Chrome storage, with optional synchronization through your signed-in Chrome profile. ICS files are generated locally in your browser. YearMap does not require a separate account and does not include extension analytics.

Yesterday's Weather

Yesterday's Weather stores the account information, saved airport-station locations, delivery preferences, and monitoring thresholds needed to provide the service. Passwords are stored as hashes. The service uses NOAA observations, National Weather Service alerts, and optional Visual Crossing snowfall data. Resend or SendGrid may process your email address to deliver messages. Basic service events may be recorded to operate and improve the product. The service does not use precise device-location tracking.

How information is used

Information is used only to provide, secure, support, and improve the functionality you request. We do not sell personal data or share it with third parties for their own advertising.

Service providers

Depending on the product, necessary processing may be performed by Google, Visual Crossing, NOAA, the National Weather Service, Render, PostgreSQL hosting, Resend, SendGrid, and basic product analytics providers. Their handling of data is governed by their respective terms and privacy policies.

Retention and your choices

  • Windblock: Use its uninstall flow, revoke Google Account access, and remove the dedicated calendar.
  • YearMap: Remove the extension and clear its Chrome storage data.
  • Yesterday's Weather: Unsubscribe using any email footer, remove saved locations, or request account deletion.

To request a copy or deletion of information associated with an account, email support@springharborstudios.com.

Children

Our products are intended for general audiences and are not directed to children under 13.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards and favor designs that limit collection. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Changes

We may update this policy as our products evolve. The revision date above will reflect the most recent material update and, where appropriate, users will be notified in-product or by email.

Contact
