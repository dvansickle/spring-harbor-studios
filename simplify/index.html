Simplify for QuickBooks® Online

Less clutter. A clearer view of your bookkeeping.

Simplify makes selected QuickBooks Online screens calmer and easier to read. Hide supported promotions, reduce visual clutter, and choose which optional controls appear.

Focus on the work

  • Cleaner layouts on supported Home, Banking, Invoices, Reports and other screens.
  • More readable tables and controls to reveal optional information.
  • Display preferences saved in your browser across companies.
  • Switch Simplify off to return to the native layout.
